# Changelog

What changed in the WaffleHub theme. The theme has no version number of its
own: update with `git pull` and `install.sh` (see the README).

## 2026-11-20

- **README** in the same order as the other Paywarr repos, with an "Updating"
  section; rebuilding, caveats and the file list moved to `docs/`.

## 2026-11-16

- **Phone menu:** a link that is in both the top bar and the dashboard menu
  (Requests, My stats, Report a problem) is shown once.
- **Poster wall:** shuffled on every page view, the same poster kept away from
  itself; WaffleHub's 18 waffle posters.

## 2026-11-15

- **A new look:** dark and streaming-style, with a poster wall on the front
  page and the sign-in pages, frosted-glass panels and a violet-to-pink accent.
- Product page: the product and its price as two cards of the same height.
- Category page: heading across the top, the categories as a menu, the
  products as cards with their description.
- The theme registers its own texts (Paymenter v1.5.9 doesn't).

## 2026-11-12 to 2026-11-13

- The first WaffleHub theme (caramel), with the Discord sign-up button on the
  register page, the Discord-only shop, and texts in English and Danish.
